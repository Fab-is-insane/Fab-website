Welcome to Your Discord Bot

Your bot is here to enhance your Discord server with various features such as music commands, moderation tools, and fun commands! It's a powerful tool that keeps your community entertained and under control.

Features:

  • Music System: Play, pause, skip, and stop music with ease.
  • Moderation: Ban, kick, mute users, and clear the chat for a clean and safe environment.
  • Fun: Get jokes, memes, quotes, and more for entertainment!

How to Use:

Simply use the available commands in your server to enjoy all the features your bot has to offer. Check out the Music, Moderation, and Fun sections for more details on how to use each feature.
